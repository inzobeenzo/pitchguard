Source: Pitch-specific tracking risk factors for UCL injury, MLB case-control study (115 surgery cases vs 230 matched controls)
 
Comparing MLB pitchers who underwent UCL reconstruction against matched controls, several
pitch-tracking metrics were associated with injury. Higher-velocity fastballs, changeups,
and sinkers were each linked to surgery. A decreased (lower, more horizontal) release point
for fastballs, curveballs, and sinkers was also associated with UCL injury. An increased
slider spin rate and a longer cutter release extension were additional risk signals. The
authors argue that the entire pitch arsenal, not fastball velocity alone, deserves attention
when assessing UCL injury risk.
