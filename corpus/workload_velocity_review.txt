Source: Systematic review of UCL injury risk factors in baseball (Journal of ISAKOS, 2021)
 
Across multiple studies, increased pitching workload and higher pitch velocity are the
most consistently reported risk factors for ulnar collateral ligament (UCL) injury in
baseball pitchers. Higher cumulative workload -- more pitches per game, inning, or season
-- was significantly associated with UCL injury in most studies examined. Pitch velocity
was the most commonly reported pitch characteristic linked to injury, with the majority of
studies finding that higher velocity was significantly associated with UCL damage. The
review noted that the definition of workload varied across studies, which makes exact
injury thresholds difficult to pin down.
