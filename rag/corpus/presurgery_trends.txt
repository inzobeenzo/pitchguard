Source: Game-by-game pitch trends before UCL reconstruction (MLB pitchers, 2009-2019)
 
A review of MLB pitchers in the 15 games leading up to Tommy John surgery looked for
patterned changes that might flag an impending injury. Pitchers showed measurable changes
in pitch selection, velocity, and spin rate in the games before surgery. Although the
absolute decreases in velocity were small, the authors note that even slight velocity drops
may reflect meaningful accumulating stress at the medial elbow. This supports tracking
within-pitcher trends over time rather than relying on single-game snapshots.
