Source: Acute-to-chronic workload ratio in injury prevention (training-load research)
 
The acute-to-chronic workload ratio (ACWR) compares an athlete's recent (acute) workload to
their longer-term (chronic) baseline workload. A sharp spike -- recent workload rising well
above the established baseline -- is associated with elevated injury risk across many sports.
Gradually building workload tends to be protective, while sudden increases overload tissues
that have not had time to adapt. Applied to pitching, a rapid jump in recent pitch volume
relative to a pitcher's own norm is a warning sign for elevated injury risk.
