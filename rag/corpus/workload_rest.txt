Source: Clinical overview of UCL injury risk factors (overhead throwers)
 
High pitch volume combined with inadequate rest between outings is a well-established
contributor to UCL injury. Repeated overuse without sufficient recovery causes microtrauma
to the ligament that accumulates faster than the tissue can heal, gradually weakening it
until it becomes prone to failure. Adequate rest days are protective. This is why pitch-count
limits and enforced recovery periods are central to UCL injury-prevention guidelines, and
why fatigue and poor recovery are considered key drivers of injury risk.
